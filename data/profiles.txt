# Linux port of the Zapret 2 NEXT profile layout (presets/_profiles.txt.in, MIT).
# The manager replaces a line that is exactly {{SECTION}} with that section of
# the active preset and fills {{LISTS}}, {{GAME_TCP}}, {{GAME_UDP}},
# {{IPSET_INCLUDE}}, {{IPSET_TCP_FILTER}}. '#' lines and empty lines are dropped.
# Ports here only pick a profile; which packets reach nfqws2 at all is decided
# by the nftables rules the manager renders next to this file.
